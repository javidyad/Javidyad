# JavidYad (جاویدیاد)

Name and tagline, approved by the owner: **جاویدیاد · به یاد جاویدنامان** (English: **JavidYad · In memory of the Javidnaman**). It matches the domain javidyad.com.

A web tool that turns a portrait photo and a name into a printable memorial poster for people killed during Iran's nationwide protests in January. It is built for the ceremony on **7 November 2026 (16 Aban) at the Paulskirche in Frankfurt**, where Prince Reza Pahlavi accepts the Friedrich Naumann Foundation's 2026 Freedom Prize on behalf of the Iranian people. People print the posters and hold them up, so they must read clearly in photos and videos.

The owner speaks Persian. Reply to him in Persian unless he writes in another language. Code, comments and commit messages stay in English.

## Hard rules

- **No red in the UI.** The owner's community is monarchist and reads red as a leftist colour. The palette is navy `#1b2a4a` and gold `#b98a1f` (dark mode: gold `#d9ae45` on navy `#1c2030`). Red inside the poster templates themselves is part of the existing design and stays.
- **Privacy is a promise shown on the page.** Photos never leave the device. No uploads, no analytics, no trackers, and no requests to any other site: fonts and jsPDF are self-hosted. Some users may be in or connected to Iran.
- **Public code, not open source.** The repo is public and the page footer links to it (`sourceNote` / `sourceLink`) so people can check the privacy promise. Do not call it "open source" and do not add an open-source licence: the owner does not want others to copy or republish it.
- **Names are English letters only** (`A-Z a-z space . ' ’ -`), max 40 characters. The name is drawn in Archivo Black.
- **Two separate language settings:**
  - UI language: English or Persian (Persian is RTL, Vazirmatn). Chosen on the first screen, switchable with the header button. Each page's strings live in `STR.en` / `STR.fa` at the top of its module; every key must exist in both.
  - Poster language: English (`Template.jpg`) or German (`Template-de.jpg`). Remembered in `localStorage` key `posterLang`.
- **Coordinates:** every number in `CONFIG` (photo box, name zone) is in the pixel space of the English `Template.jpg`. Any template is stretched to that size (`design.w/h`), so a new template only needs the same aspect ratio and layout. If `Template.jpg` is ever replaced with a different size, scale `CONFIG` with it.
- **German text must be checked by a native speaker** before printing. Current copy: `SAGT ES DER WELT` / `IM IRAN GETÖTET` / `BEI DEN LANDESWEITEN PROTESTEN IM JANUAR` / `FRANKFURT • 7. NOVEMBER`.
- The red German line must stay the same height as the English one (it was once made smaller and the owner rejected it: posters are read from a distance).

## How the page behaves

1. Language screen (emblem, English / فارسی), centred.
2. Form: poster language, "choose from the Javidnam list" button (only when the list has people) or the chosen person's banner, photo picker (with privacy note), preview, zoom slider, name, PDF paper size (A3/A4), buttons.
   A `?lang=en|fa` link (used by the list page) skips the language screen; `&p=<id>` fills in that person. Reset or picking an own photo drops `p` from the address.
3. Before any photo or name, the preview shows a small sample poster with a ghosted photo frame and `Firstname Lastname`. These placeholders are preview-only and never exported.
4. Photo: drag to move, pinch / wheel / slider to zoom (1–3×), arrow keys and +/- when the preview has focus. Default framing sits 30 % from the top (`focusY`) so faces are not cut.
5. Output:
   - **Save to gallery**: share sheet with a ~9 MP JPEG; falls back to a full-screen image the user long-presses to save (in-app browsers).
   - **Download JPG**: full size, capped at 16 MP for iOS.
   - **Download print PDF**: A3 or A4, image centred.

## Working on it

- No build step, no framework: plain HTML, CSS and ES modules. Open the pages through a local server (`python -m http.server`), not `file://` (modules and canvas export need a server).
- After changing anything in `css/` or `js/`, run `python tools/bump_version.py`. GitHub Pages lets browsers keep files for 10 minutes, and the new `?v=` stops a fresh page from running an older script.
- Before every pull request, run `node tests/smoke.js` (CI runs it too). When a change adds a feature or a path people use, extend the test.
- Work on a branch and merge through a pull request; `main` is what the site serves.
- Test on a real iPhone and Android phone before publishing anything user-facing, including from inside Instagram / Telegram in-app browsers.
- Freeze changes around 3 November 2026; after that only fixes.

## Updating the Javidnam list

Source: Iran International's Javidnaman platform (https://javidnaman.iranintl.com/), used with the owner's permission and credited on `javidnam.html`. The platform has Persian names only, and its "place" field is the place of death (often a street or neighbourhood).

1. `python tools/fetch_javidnaman.py --out <scratch>` downloads the platform's list and every photo that passes the owner's rule: **width and height both at least 1000 px** (smaller photos are never used). It writes `<scratch>/candidates.csv`.
2. Add new people to `javidnam/source.csv` (columns: English name, Persian name, English province, Persian province, English city, Persian city, photo id). Conventions:
   - English names follow common Iranian spelling (Mohammad, Hossein, Mehdi, Hassan, Reza). Compound first names are split ("Mohammad Sadegh", "Amir Hossein", "Ali Reza"). Surname suffixes join the word before them (Hassanzadeh, Salehpour). Nicknames in brackets stay in the Persian name only.
   - City: the city or town, not the street. Neighbourhoods go under their city (Narmak → Tehran, Gohardasht → Karaj). Towns that are cities in their own right keep their own name (Najafabad, Shahr-e Qods); the owner counts Fardis and Meshkindasht as Karaj. Leave both city columns empty when the place gives no city ("city not recorded"), and the province columns too when it gives no province ("place not recorded"). Every city needs its province.
3. `python tools/build_javidnam.py --csv javidnam/source.csv --photos <scratch>/photos --out javidnam/` checks the sheet and writes the site files.

Only resized, EXIF-free copies go in the repo. A wrong photo or misspelled name is very hurtful to families, so let the owner review before publishing.
